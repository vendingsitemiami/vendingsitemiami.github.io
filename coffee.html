Frequently Asked Questions

Which machine do you use?

Every coffee machine we place is an Azkoyen Vitro S5, a bean-to-cup espresso machine built for busy workplaces.

How big is it and how much does it weigh?

It measures 18.9 in wide, 23.3 in deep, and 30.5 in tall, and weighs about 115 lb (52 kg). It sits on a sturdy countertop.

How do I pay?

Tap, dip, or swipe a credit or debit card, or use Apple Pay or Google Pay, at the machine's 365 Vending Castles card reader.

Does it use fresh milk?

No. Cappuccinos, lattes, and other milk drinks use soluble milk, so there is no fridge to keep stocked.

Is the coffee machine really free?

Yes. No upfront costs or service fees.

How do I request one?

Fill out the request form or call (305) 615-0817.
