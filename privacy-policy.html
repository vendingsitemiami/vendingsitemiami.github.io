Privacy Policy

Last updated July 29, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Service Amenities ("Service Amenities," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit serviceamenities.com or market.serviceamenities.com, or otherwise interact with our vending and micro market services.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide. When you submit a form (such as a "Request a Market" inquiry), we may collect your name, business name, email address, phone number, business address, and any message you include.

Usage data. Like most websites, our servers and analytics tools may automatically collect information such as pages visited and referring URLs.

Payment information. Purchases at a vending machine or micro market kiosk are processed by the payment terminal on that device through our payment processor, not by this website. We do not directly collect or store full payment card numbers. The kiosk or payment device may share with us a card identifier, not the full card number, that we use to restrict purchases from that card at a machine — for example, if the card is associated with blocking a vending machine's camera or other unauthorized interference with the machine during a purchase.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests for a vending or micro market installation.
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our website and services.
  • Communicate with you about your orders or service updates.
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends to improve our services.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, security, or technical issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences (such as light/dark mode) and understand how our website is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site functionality.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate our website and services, such as our form-processing provider (FormSubmit).
  • Our payment processor, that could vary based on machine or market type, solely to facilitate transactions at vending and micro market kiosks.
  • Professional advisors, or authorities, where required by law or to protect our rights.
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, or to opt out of certain communications. To make a request, contact us at info@serviceamenities.com. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business use and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to third-party websites we do not control. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to review their policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
