Accessibility Statement

Last updated July 29, 2026

1. Our Commitment

Service Amenities is committed to making serviceamenities.com and market.serviceamenities.com accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe access to information about our vending and micro market services should not depend on the technology or ability of the person browsing.

2. Conformance Standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities.

3. Measures We Take

Steps we take to support accessibility on our website include:

Sufficient color contrast between text and background in both light and dark mode.
Descriptive labels on interactive controls, including navigation and back buttons.
Semantic HTML structure with clear headings to support screen reader navigation.
A responsive, mobile-safe layout that supports browser zoom and text resizing.

4. Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of our website may not yet be fully accessible. Known limitations may include third-party embedded content or forms that have not been fully tested against WCAG 2.1 AA. We are working to identify and address these issues over time.

5. Third-Party Content

Our website relies on some third-party tools, such as our form processing provider. While we select vendors that support accessibility, we do not directly control the accessibility of their interfaces.

6. Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of serviceamenities.com and market.serviceamenities.com. If you encounter an accessibility barrier or have suggestions for improvement, please let us know:

7. Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As we update our website and add new features, we review them for accessibility and welcome continued feedback to help us improve.
